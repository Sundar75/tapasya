# 🎨 Tapasya Website – Bharatanatyam & Carnatic Music Academy

A responsive website for an **Indian classical art school** based in Chennai, dedicated to **Bharatanatyam** and **Carnatic music**. The site highlights the academy’s cultural heritage, provides course details, showcases events, and supports **online enrollment for students worldwide**.  

---

## ✨ Features
- 📖 **About Us** – Story of the academy, faculty, and legacy.  
- 🕉️ **Courses** – Details on Bharatanatyam and Carnatic music programs.  
- 🌍 **Online Classes** – Virtual learning options for international students.  
- 📅 **Events & Performances** – Updates on recitals, workshops, and festivals.  
- 🖼️ **Gallery** – Photos and videos of performances and cultural activities.  
- 📞 **Contact & Enrollment** – Easy-to-use form for inquiries and registration.
